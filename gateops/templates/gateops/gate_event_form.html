Arrival checklist

  1. Phone: use the visitor's mobile to deduplicate against existing persons.
  2. Name: required when creating a new person record.
  3. Visitor category: drives the rule engine match (e.g. DELIVERY, GUEST).
  4. Gate & direction: record which gate and whether the visitor is inbound or outbound.
  5. Rule engine: after saving, rules decide auto-approve, reject, or pending approval.
A blacklisted person can never be auto-approved — the event is held for manual approval regardless of rule outcome.
